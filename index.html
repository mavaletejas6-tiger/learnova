Scanning trusted free sources across the internet…

Free learning exists, but it is scattered.

Students know free classes are out there. Finding them is the hard part.

🗂️

Too many places

Free courses sit on many websites and YouTube channels. You keep opening new tabs and still miss good ones.

🔒

Hidden paywalls

Some courses look free, then ask for payment before the useful part. Wasting time and data hurts.

📶

Hard to reach

Many classes are only in English. Many need fast internet or a costly phone. That leaves out a lot of learners.

Roadmap

A simple, honest plan for the first six months.

Months 1–2

Phase 1: Build

  • Focus on one niche subject first
  • Hand-pick 100–200 free courses and classes
  • Ship search with language and level filters
Months 3–4

Phase 2: Test

  • Try it with 50–100 real students
  • Fix whatever breaks on low-end phones
  • Add a second language
Months 5–6

Phase 3: Scale

  • Automated scanning of trusted free sources
  • More topics and more languages
  • Low-data mode and offline saved lists

Always free for learners
