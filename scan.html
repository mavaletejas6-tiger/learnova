Preparing scan…

Google web ↗

    Every card below came from a request made when you searched. A free AI model (LLM7, with Pollinations as backup) writes the search queries; your browser then fetches real results from YouTube, edX, Coursera, DuckDuckGo’s open web index, GitHub, PeerTube, Wikiversity / Wikibooks and DEV Community. The AI never supplies links — it only plans searches. Cards marked FREE come from sources that only hold free material; cards marked FREE TO AUDIT come from edX or Coursera, where the lessons are usually free but graded work and certificates may be paid — check the page. Cards marked OPEN WEB come from the wider web or academic sites, so check the page is free before you enrol. Language and level are read from the source when it reports them — results whose source reports a different language or level are filtered out, and anything a source does not report stays visible and unlabelled rather than guessed. Every link opens the original creator’s page — we never copy or re-host anyone’s content.

    What we actually scan

    Learnova keeps a hand-checked catalogue, and this page adds a live web scan on top of it. Every search runs in your browser, against these sources:

    • YouTube
    • edX
    • Coursera
    • Open web (DuckDuckGo)
    • GitHub
    • PeerTube
    • Wikiversity
    • Wikibooks
    • DEV Community

    Those indexes reach platforms and creators everywhere, so a search also surfaces NPTEL, SWAYAM, Khan Academy, MIT OpenCourseWare, freeCodeCamp, OpenStax, university pages — plus independent teachers who publish for free. edX and Coursera courses are found through DuckDuckGo’s index of their public course pages and labelled FREE TO AUDIT, because their lessons are usually free while certificates can be paid. A free AI plans the queries first, but every card still links to the original creator with credit. We never copy or re-host anyone’s content.

    • Low-data mode and offline saved lists
